His history

Prince Rogers Nelson was born in Minneapolis in 1958. On his first album, For You (1978), he sang and played the instruments himself.

In 1984, Prince and the Revolution released Purple Rain. Prince also starred in the movie of the same name. The soundtrack paired dance songs like “Let’s Go Crazy” with the long guitar solo of the title track.

Prince kept recording and performing for decades. In 2007 he played “Purple Rain” during a rainy Super Bowl halftime show. He died in 2016.
