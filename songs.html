Top songs

Five songs to start with, in release order.

  • 1999 (1982) — A party anthem built around a repeating keyboard riff.
  • When Doves Cry (1984) — Listen for the space around the drums and vocals.
  • Let’s Go Crazy (1984) — A spoken opening gives way to a fast rock groove.
  • Purple Rain (1984) — A slow ballad that builds to a soaring guitar solo.
  • Kiss (1986) — A clipped guitar rhythm leaves room for his high voice.
